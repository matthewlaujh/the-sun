## Assembly notes (JLCPCB)

- Single-side SMT assembly (top = LED side): LEDs + 100 nF caps + series R. Wire-side parts (connectors, bulk caps) are THT/hand-solder — or flip to standard double-side assembly if you want them placed too.
- SK6812 RGBW is moisture-sensitive (MSL); JLCPCB bakes automatically — no action.
- Confirm the LED part's PCBA process (economic vs standard) on its JLCPCB page before committing the panel size.
- Mounting hardware: NONE on these boards by design (added in a later revision).
