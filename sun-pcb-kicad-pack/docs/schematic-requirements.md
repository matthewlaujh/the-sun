# Schematic design requirements

Project: LED sun, Ø1773 mm disc · concentric rings, 12 wedges + centre disc · SK6812 RGBW @ 30 mm pitch · 2416 LEDs on 25 boards (3 unique designs) · PixLite E4-S Mk3.
Generated by Sun PCB Studio. Companion files: design.json (machine-readable), designs/*-outline.dxf, designs/*-placement.csv.

## Architecture (one schematic per design; 3 schematics total)

Each board is electrically identical in topology: a 5 V pixel chain with pass-through data.

1. **LED array** — SK6812 RGBW, 4-pin 5050 (VDD, DOUT, GND, DIN). Draw as a repeated block or scripted array; reference designators MUST be LED1…LEDn in chain order (placement CSV order).
2. **Decoupling** — one 100 nF X7R 0603 across VDD–GND at EVERY LED, placed ≤ 2 mm from the package (cap side alternates with serpentine rows, as modelled). Refs C1…Cn, Ck belongs to LEDk.
3. **Data entry** — 3-pin data-in connector (J_DIN): +5V sense optional, GND, DATA. A 100 Ω series resistor (R1) between J_DIN.DATA and LED1.DIN, placed at LED1. One per board.
4. **Data exit** — 3-pin data-out connector (J_DOUT) fed from the last LED's DOUT. Boards daisy-chain J_DOUT → next board's J_DIN in the order in boards.csv / dmx-patch.csv.
5. **Power injection** — per board: the number of feed connectors listed below, each rated ≥ 10 A at 5 V (suggest M4 ring-lug pads or XT30PW; wire side). Every feed lands on the same +5V/GND pours — feeds are parallel, not split rails.
6. **Bulk capacitance** — ≥ 470–1000 µF low-ESR electrolytic/polymer at EACH power feed, plus one 22–47 µF ceramic per ~30 LEDs distributed along the array.
7. **No level shifter** — the PixLite drives 5 V data directly. Keep board-to-board data pigtails short (< 150 mm) and pair DATA with GND in the loom.
