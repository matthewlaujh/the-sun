## System power

- 2416 × SK6812 RGBW @ 80 mA full white = **193 A @ 5 V (966 W)** worst case; typical content is far lower, but size for full white.
- PSU budget: ≥ 1160 W of 5 V supply (20 % headroom), e.g. 4× 300 W-class 5 V units, chassis-mounted behind the disc, short feeders.
- Distribution: one fused feeder per board power feed (blade fuse, rate ≈ 1.25× feed load, ≤ 10 A). 16 AWG per 10 A feeder max ~1 m.
- All GNDs common: PSU V− bus ↔ every board ↔ controller GND (PixLite outputs reference GND).

*IPC-2221 external, 10 °C rise, fed both ends. **Don't route power as traces — pour it**: V+ pour and GND pour covering the wire side (split roughly half/half or V+ pour + GND on LED side under the serpentine), stitched with vias at every LED. Wedge boards: run the pours as arcs following the LED rings; feed at the outer edge.

- Voltage-drop target: ≤ 0.15 V board-internal at full white (LEDs dim/redshift below ~4.5 V). With full-face pours this is comfortably met; verify worst path in the wedge tips.
- Data: 800 kHz single-ended; impedance uncontrolled. Keep LED-to-LED hops direct (serpentine per routing SVG), 100 Ω series at each board entry, pair with GND between boards.
- Frame budget: worst output chain 38.7 fps at 745 px/output cap (target 33.2).
- Thermal: 966 W over 2.5 m² is fine in open air at typical content; sustained full white pushes LED junctions — cap master brightness at ~60 % in the controller unless the show needs more.
