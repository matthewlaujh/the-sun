- ✓Fixing BOM: 264× SMTSO3080MTJ (JLCPCB C2916368, M3 × 8 mm SMT standoff, tin-plated steel) reflowed on the wire side at PCBA — order a few % spare — plus 264× M3 screws driven from the frame side (length ≈ member wall + 5–6 mm engagement, e.g. M3×8 for 2–3 mm sheet). Nothing sits on the LED face at all, and the 8 mm standoff height is your wiring gap between panel backs and the frame. Pad Ø6 mm is an assumption — JLCPCB doesn't publish the footprint; confirm against the EasyEDA footprint before fab.
- ✓Standoffs clear the LED face completely: the rim is sized only by the locating hole and its data-trace keepout (8.3 mm), so the channel can be 13.5 mm at this pitch where printed clips would allow only 3 mm — and no screw head or clip flange exists anywhere on the LED side.
- ✓Fabrication: the wheel is Ø1,773 mm — wider than standard 1250/1500 mm sheet — so laser-cut it as 2 halves or 4 quadrants with bolted splice plates (put splices mid-spoke, not on hoops), or weld it from profiles (hoops rolled or nested from sheet, spokes from straight stock). Flat sheet is very stiff in-plane (fine hanging vertically); if the sun ever lies flat, use the boxed section above rather than flat bar. Rule of thumb: 3 mm aluminium or 2 mm steel sheet for a vertical hang.
- ✓Every standoff locating hole clears the nearest LED pad by 9.7 mm (needs 5.8 mm); the Ø6 mm solder pad lives on the wire side, so it can't touch an LED at all.
- ✓Centre disc auto-sized by DMX + JLCPCB: Ø450 mm round board, 172 LEDs, 2 universe(s).
- ✓Open channel 2 mm wide at every seam (max 13.5 mm at this pitch) — board edges sit 14 mm from LED centres, so LED spacing across a seam is still exactly 30 mm. Clips reach 9 mm onto each rim, clear of every pad and cap, and the channels stay open for access.
- ✓Drill patterns are identical on every board of each design (3 fab designs incl. mounting holes) — one drill file per design.
- ✓No mounting hole touches the LED data serpentine (holes are placed to clear every hop by ≥ 3.9 mm).
- ✓No LED can sit off-board or on a seam: every board edge is pitch/2 from LED centres (rings-mode band cuts snap mid-way between LED rings) → 11.5 mm package→edge everywhere (≥ 3 mm keepout).
- ✓Every PCB has ≥3 standoffs (each board hangs on its own standoffs off the frame).
- ✓Biggest PCB: 172 px = 13.8 A full white → 2 feed(s) at 10 A. Bus width ≥ 2.2 mm per rail (2 oz, fed both ends, 10 °C rise) — use full copper pours on the wire side.

## File guide

config.json — reload via "Load config". led-map.csv — one row per LED in data-chain order. dmx-patch.csv — universe/board/output patch plan. designs/ — per unique PCB design (letter matches the app): placement CSV (board-local + global coords, rotation for ring LEDs), drill CSV (standoff locating holes), routing SVG (serpentine, rails, hardware at true size). boards.csv — every physical board with its design letter, location and rotation for assembly. skeleton-cutlist.csv — orderable frame pieces (straight lengths + arc radii); skeleton-members.csv — every member's coordinates and role (seam / spoke); standoffs.csv — every SMTSO3080MTJ position (add these to each design's PCBA BOM). In clip mode instead: spacers.csv — every printed stick's endpoints; ring-anchors.csv — where ring-tie sticks bolt to the outer ring (build tabs there).
